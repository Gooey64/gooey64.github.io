What kinds of projects do I like?

Games as modular systems: I follow the League of Legends pro scene, and what hooks me is how those games are put together: champions and items are components with their own rules, and the interesting part is how those rules interact. I've built platformers in Unity, and on Owlhead I integrated a treadmill so you can walk through a game with your own feet.

Robots because they're cool: I like code that makes hardware move, like training a LEGO quadruped to walk or connecting a classroom of robots to a teacher's phone. Next up is a 5th-year M.S. in Human-Robot Interaction.

Projects with impacts on communities: I enjoy building backends and tools for actual users in my community, like the dishware-lending app for the Lexington Zero Waste nonprofit.
